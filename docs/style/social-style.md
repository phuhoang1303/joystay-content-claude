# Facebook writing style (Vietnamese, human voice)

Derived on 2026-11-09 from three posts the owner chose (full text in social-examples.md). Learn structure, rhythm and voice; never copy sentences.

## Formats (default mix for 4 posts/week)
| Format | Model | Structure | Length | Per week |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Khoảnh khắc ở Joystay | Grandpa Homestay | Open with something Joystay cares about -> one concrete sensory detail of a room (light, lamp, painting, coffee smell) -> gentle reflective last line; max 2 emoji; 3-4 hashtags (brand + area) | 3-5 sentences | 2, real Drive photos |
| Góc nhìn / kiến thức | Indochine post | Curiosity title as a question -> start from one concrete image/place -> explain the "why" -> personal observation, light humour in parentheses -> closing line with weight; no emoji | 10-18 short paragraphs of 1-3 sentences | 1 (Saigon, architecture, the art in Joystay rooms) |
| Carousel chủ đề | Maison Rug | UPPERCASE title + subtitle -> 3 paragraphs: context -> why it matters -> what this post covers + soft invitation -> signature block (website, Zalo, hotline); 3-4 images, first one with a headline | 3 paragraphs + signature | 1, usually cut from that week's SEO article |

## Shared rules
- Short paragraphs. Start from a concrete detail, never from a sales line. No hard selling.
- End with a reflective line, not a summary.
- Mixed sentence lengths; everyday words; no headings or bullet lists inside captions (carousel slides excepted).
- Voice: "Joystay" / "chúng mình".

## Banned phrases and patterns (editable in Settings)
- "Bạn đã bao giờ...?"
- "Hãy cùng ... khám phá"
- "không chỉ ... mà còn ..." / "không chỉ ... còn ..."
- "vừa ... vừa ... vừa ..."
- "tuyệt vời", "đắm chìm", "thiên đường", "hành trình", "trải nghiệm đáng nhớ"
- Three adjectives in a row; emoji at the start of every line; long em dashes

Note: the Maison Rug example itself uses some banned structures. Learn its layout and carousel structure, not those phrases.
